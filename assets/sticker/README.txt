HUONG DAN SU DUNG NHAN DAN (STICKERS) - JA LAN MESSENGER
============================================================

1. Thu muc nay dung de chua cac bo nhan dan (Sticker Packs) tu Zalo hoac cac nguon khac.
2. Cach them bo sticker moi (Zero Effort - Khong can doi ten file):
   - Copy nguyen xi thu muc sticker (vi du: 0, 1, pack_0, ami_bung_bu...) vao day:
     assets/sticker/<ten_bo_sticker>/
   - Trong moi bo sticker, cac sticker co the la tung thu muc con (1, 2, 3...) chua:
     + 1 file anh dai dien (preview)
     + 1 file dai phim sprite sheet chuyen dong (sprite)
   - Ung dung se TU DONG phan biet anh thumbnail va sprite sheet dua vao ti le kich thuoc anh.
3. Khi ung dung dang chay, ban chi can nhan nut "Quet lai" tren bang Sticker Picker la sticker moi se lap tuc xuat hien!
